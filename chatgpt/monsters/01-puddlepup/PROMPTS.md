- Markings: cream belly, chest and muzzle; darker-teal dapple patches in the fur over the back and the top of the head.
- Colours: fur teal #3FB6A8 · belly, chest and muzzle cream #F3E6C4 · ear membranes, tail fin edge, toe pads and blush pink #F28FA8 · dapples darker teal #36A698 · eyes (iris) dark slate #24323A.

Show: Puddlepup standing on all four feet in a relaxed, happy idle, head turned a little toward the viewer, a small happy smile, in a three-quarter front view (turned about 30 degrees), the whole creature in frame and centred, with clear space around it and a soft contact shadow, on the plain grey background.
Render it as a premium, highly detailed stylized 3D character, with special care on the damp, clumpy fur with its wet sheen, the ribbed pink fin ears, the webbed paws and the crisp dapples. Zoomed in, every surface the card describes should show its real texture, never smooth plastic.
Avoid: looking like any famous cartoon dog, any famous water-type starter creature, and any famous axolotl character. No text.
